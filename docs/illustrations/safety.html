Heat guard steps in before the projector cuts out

The stock OS throttles quietly and switches off at 115 °C with no warning. Lumen watches the CPU and GPU and acts at three earlier points.
CPU limits shown for the HY300 (its firmware switches off at 115 °C; the GPU at 110 °C, where Lumen acts 5 °C earlier). On other projectors Lumen reads the chip's own limits. If heat ever does switch it off, the next start says so.
